# KaziScout Contributor Licence Agreement

_Draft version 0.1, 2026-10-06. This is a working draft written by the maintainer, not by a
lawyer. Have it reviewed before relying on it for a commercial licence deal._

Thank you for contributing to KaziScout. This agreement records the terms under which you give
your contributions to the project. It exists for one reason: KaziScout is published under
AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the maintainer also offers commercial licences to organisations that
cannot use AGPL software. That is only possible if the maintainer has the right to license every
part of the project, including the parts you write.

You keep the copyright in your contribution. You are giving a licence, not handing over ownership.

- **"You"** means the person or organisation submitting a contribution.
- **"Maintainer"** means Benard Kimani, the copyright holder of KaziScout, and any successor who
  takes over the project.
- **"Contribution"** means any code, documentation or other material you submit to the KaziScout

to use, copy, modify, publish, distribute and sublicense your Contribution, and to license it to
others under any terms, including terms that are not open source.

If you hold patents that your Contribution necessarily infringes, you grant the Maintainer and
everyone who receives KaziScout a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence under those patents
to use the Contribution as part of KaziScout.

## 3. What the Maintainer promises in return

The Maintainer will keep publishing KaziScout, including your Contribution, under AGPL-3.0-or-later
or another licence approved by the Open Source Initiative. A commercial licence is offered in
addition to the open-source one, never instead of it.

## 4. What you confirm

- The Contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it under these terms.
- If your employer has rights to work you create, you have its permission, or it has waived
  those rights for this Contribution.
- Where the Contribution includes someone else's work, you have said so clearly in the pull
  request and that work is under a licence compatible with AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- You are not required to provide support. The Contribution is given as it is, without warranty.

## 5. How to agree

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

> I have read the KaziScout Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA.md, version 0.1) and I agree to it.

That statement covers the pull request it appears in and every later Contribution you make,
until a new version of this agreement asks you to confirm again.
